# A new home for the blog's community posts

From now on the docs blog carries translations, technical analysis and regional observations only. The 31 posts about events, project launches, progress reports and the community's positions have moved to the [community updates](https://anoni.net/en/updates/){target="_blank"} on the home site, which is also where future community announcements will appear.

<!-- more -->

## Old addresses and RSS

Old addresses redirect to the posts' new locations, on the onion service too, so bookmarks and links in other articles keep working.

If you follow the docs blog by RSS, you will now get content posts only. To keep hearing about events and projects, also subscribe to the [community updates feed](https://anoni.net/en/updates/feed.xml){target="_blank"} or the [newsletter](https://anoni.net/en/contact/){target="_blank"}.

## What moved

| Kind | Posts | For example |
|---|---|---|
| Event announcements and recaps | 13 | The COSCUP 2026 track, Global Gathering 2026, RightsCon 2025 |
| Project launches | 5 | anoni.net/news, CryptPad in Traditional Chinese, the campus relay proposal kit |
| Progress reports | 8 | Monthly project updates, the 2025 review and 2026 plans |
| Positions and experience | 5 | Why we self-host Matrix, the Tor relay at NTNU |

The updates list on the home site tags each entry as New, Event or Update. The move is documented in [anoni-net/www#42](https://github.com/anoni-net/www/issues/42){target="_blank"}.

# New Home for the Blog's Technical Posts

The docs site is written for people new to anonymity networks and privacy, who may not yet know what Tor is. Posts that only make sense once you know Tor, cryptography or network measurement have moved to the new [Articles](https://anoni.net/en/articles/){target="_blank"} section on the anoni.net home site. From now on this blog carries translations, analysis and regional observations written for a general audience.

<!-- more -->

## What Moved

| Content | New home |
|---|---|
| Decentralized website publishing, post-quantum cryptography, and zero-knowledge identity and payments from the Advanced section, plus the end-to-end encryption protocols | [Articles](https://anoni.net/en/articles/){target="_blank"} on the home site |
| End-to-end encryption and networks mistaken for anonymity | Stay on the docs site under [Concepts](../../basics/index.md); the encryption page is now [The Scope of End-to-End Encryption](../../basics/e2ee.md) |
| The OONI Run v2 census, the 13 August mobile throttle measurements, faulty OONI measurements, stateless Tor relays, Unredacted, the Tor sysadmin team's ADRs, and the technical design of offline reading | [Articles](https://anoni.net/en/articles/){target="_blank"} on the home site |
| The call to measure the throttle drill, the launch of the interactive section, and the launch notes for the two report translations | [Updates](https://anoni.net/en/updates/){target="_blank"} on the home site |
| Release notes for older Arti and Tails versions | Folded into the [changelogs](../../changelog/index.md) |
| Notes on the Cabinet draft of Taiwan's Virtual Asset Service Act | Folded into [Taiwan's 2026 Virtual Asset Service Act](../../regional/taiwan-vasp-2026.md) |

The report index is now called [Translated Reports](../../reports/index.md), and the editorial observation on the MADLink report has become a community update, kept as a snapshot from May 2026.

## Old Addresses

Old addresses redirect to the new ones, on the onion site as well, so bookmarks and links in other articles keep working. The change is tracked in [toomore/anoni-net-ws#406](https://github.com/toomore/anoni-net-ws/issues/406){target="_blank"}.

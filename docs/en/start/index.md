# :material-sign-direction: Start by role

This site has over two hundred pages, and it is hard to tell on a first visit which ones apply to you. This section gives each role one starting path: three pages for a twenty-minute pass, roughly ten more for a week. The developer path splits by what you already have, so its shape differs from the other four.

Every link points at a page that already exists here. Nothing on these five pages is new material.

!!! info "What this site adds, and what it does not"

    For general digital-safety how-to written for a global audience, [EFF Surveillance Self-Defense](https://ssd.eff.org/){target="_blank"} and [Privacy Guides](https://www.privacyguides.org/){target="_blank"} are well-established and we link to them rather than duplicating them. What the paths below add is the regional layer: how a tool behaves across the Sinophone Asia-Pacific, what a local statute changes about your threat model, and measurement from inside the region.

    Regulatory pages are Taiwan-specific unless stated otherwise — Taiwan is the only jurisdiction where this community has first-hand standing. Treat them as worked examples and check your own jurisdiction.

## Five entry points

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- :material-account-group:{ .lg .middle style="color: var(--brand-cyan-500);" } **Civil society**

    ---

    Staff and long-term volunteers at advocacy groups, service NGOs, and research organizations. Data inventory, anonymous tip channels, anonymous donations, and data-protection duties.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Civil society path](./civil-society.md)

- :material-newspaper-variant-outline:{ .lg .middle style="color: var(--cat-privacy);" } **Newsrooms**

    ---

    Organizations with an editorial desk, legal counsel, and IT. Public intake channels, material retention and disclosure requests, verification that does not burn the source.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Newsroom path](./media.md)

- :material-account-edit-outline:{ .lg .middle style="color: var(--cat-privacy);" } **Independent journalists**

    ---

    Freelancers, one-person outlets, citizen journalists. Compartmentalizing a single device, running an intake channel alone, preparing for border crossings.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Independent journalist path](./independent-journalist.md)

- :material-console:{ .lg .middle style="color: var(--brand-cyan-500);" } **Open-source developers**

    ---

    People who run servers, write code, work with data, or write documentation. Running nodes, measurement analysis, campus deployments, and documentation: four lines you can pick up.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Developer path](./developer.md)

- :material-account-outline:{ .lg .middle style="color: var(--cat-privacy);" } **General readers**

    ---

    No specific adversary, you would just rather not be profiled by platforms and ad networks. Starts with the lowest-cost changes, and is the shared baseline under the other four roles.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: General reader path](./everyone.md)

</div>

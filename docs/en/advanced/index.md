# :material-lock-outline: Advanced

After the concepts and the tools, this section goes into subjects with more technical depth. The material leans toward principles and design choices, and suits readers asking why a tool is built the way it is, or where things are heading over the next decade. The pages keep the rigour without stacking up terminology, and use examples and diagrams where they help.

## Articles

- [Post-quantum cryptography](./post-quantum.md): the NIST standards, the harvest-now-decrypt-later threat model, and how far real systems have got.
- [Decentralized website publishing](./dweb-ipfs-onion.md): what IPFS and onion services each solve, and how this site deploys to both.
- [Zero-knowledge identity and payments](./zk-identity-payments.md): ring signatures, zk-SNARKs, what chain analysis can and cannot do, and proving an attribute without revealing it.
- [Networks mistaken for anonymity](./mistaken-for-anonymity.md): what encrypted DNS, IPFS, Yggdrasil, DN42, and I2P each provide, and what they do not.
